Floating: `<fw-popover>` (click, hover, focus or manual trigger, optional arrow), `<fw-tooltip>` (delayed on hover, immediate on keyboard focus, text mirrored to `aria-description`) and `<fw-dropdown>` with `<fw-menu-item>`, `<fw-menu-divider>` and `<fw-menu-label>` (checkbox and radio items, typeahead, the full menu button keyboard pattern). All open in the top layer and flip at the viewport edge.
