Adds a `toast` kind: a passing notice that stacks in a corner, announces itself politely, dismisses on a timer, and never takes focus or locks the page. `overlay.toast({ text, tone, duration, position })`; `duration: 0` keeps it up for a "working…" notice with no known end.
