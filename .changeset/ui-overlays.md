Overlays: `<fw-dialog>` and `<fw-drawer>` built on the native `<dialog>` (focus containment, inert page, scroll lock, a cancelable `fw-request-close` for Escape, backdrop and close button), and `<fw-toast>` with `<fw-toast-region>` plus a `showToast()` helper. Toasts pause while hovered or focused and show above an open modal dialog.
