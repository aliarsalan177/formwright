Form controls: `<fw-textarea>` (auto-resize, character counter), `<fw-checkbox>` (indeterminate), `<fw-switch>`, `<fw-radio-group>` with `<fw-radio>` (arrow keys move and select, one tab stop), and `<fw-slider>` (value kept within `min`/`max` and on `step`). All submit with a native form, validate with `required`, reset with the form and follow a disabled `<fieldset>`.
